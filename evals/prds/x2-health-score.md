# Customer Health Score PRD

## Problem
Our customer success team needs better visibility into which accounts are healthy. Right now it's hard to know who is at risk, and CSMs are frustrated. A health score would help everyone.

## Success metrics
- CSMs are happier with their tools.
- Fewer accounts churn.

## Proposed idea
Build a health score from 0 to 100 for every account, shown on the account page and in a new dashboard. It should combine usage, support tickets and NPS. We can use machine learning to make it smarter over time.

## Scope
**Non-goals:** Automated outreach to at-risk accounts.

## Rollout
Ship to the CS team first, then make it visible to sales.
