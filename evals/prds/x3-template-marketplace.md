# Template Marketplace (idea)

What if users could publish and share their project templates with each other? We have a lot of power users who build great setups, and newer users struggle to get started. A marketplace could help both groups and might become a growth channel, since templates could be shared publicly and indexed by search engines.

Some thoughts:
- Could start free and later let creators charge for premium templates.
- Maybe feature the best templates on the home page.
- Might need some kind of moderation.

Would love feedback on whether this is worth exploring next quarter.
